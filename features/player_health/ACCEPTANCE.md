# Acceptance — Player Health

- [ ] Player health starts at 100 / 100.
- [ ] Positive damage lowers health by the requested amount.
- [ ] Damage cannot reduce health below zero.
- [ ] Positive healing raises health by the requested amount.
- [ ] Healing cannot raise health above maximum health.
- [ ] Zero or negative damage/healing has no effect.
- [ ] Reaching zero emits `died` exactly once.
- [ ] A dead component ignores later damage and healing.
- [ ] `health_changed` reports initial health and subsequent changes.
- [ ] The project imports and launches without parser/runtime errors.
- [ ] No HUD, game-over flow, enemy, combat, potion, or debug control was added.
