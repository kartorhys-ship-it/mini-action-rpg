# Acceptance — Training Dummy

- [ ] A wooden dummy is visible in the room near the player's starting position.
- [ ] The dummy starts at 100 / 100 and its health label is readable.
- [ ] One sword hit reduces the dummy to 75 / 100.
- [ ] A single swing cannot damage the dummy multiple times.
- [ ] The dummy's health readout updates when health changes.
- [ ] At zero health the dummy remains in place and displays `DUMMY DOWN`.
- [ ] The dummy does not move or attack the player.
- [ ] The full main scene imports and runs without parser/runtime errors.
- [ ] No player HUD, enemy AI, item drops, or unrelated feature was added.
